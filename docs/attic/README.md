# The attic — designs this game is no longer making

Kept, not deleted, because every one of these was load-bearing for weeks and a
future session that cannot see them will re-derive them from scratch. That has
already happened twice in this repo.

**Nothing in here is a requirement. Nothing in here should be cited as one.**
`docs/FRONTIER.md` is the design. `docs/BRIEF.md` governs above it, minus asks 8
and 9.

## Why it is all in one box

The owner, 2026-08-02: *"please put all the AI ideas and shit to stash
somewhere, just your typical low fantasy with no twist."*

Two things ended on that line and they are worth telling apart:

- **The AI premise** — the player turns out to be a model travelling a graph to
  learn. `VISION.md`, `PIVOT.md`, `RESET.md`, `GLOSSARY.md`, `CONCEPTS.tsv`,
  `SIMPLIFICATIONS.md` and most of `DIRECTIONS.md` are built on it.
- **Any twist at all.** After the AI reveal was cut, the design briefly grew a
  replacement — the chief engineer's plan turning out to be a reissue of an
  older survey, the drove wall being the last road gone back to grass. The owner
  cut that too. **The game has no secret.** See `FRONTIER.md` §11.

⚠️ **`DIRECTIONS.md` is the one worth reading anyway.** Its central finding is
premise-independent and still true: *shuffle which node connects to which, keep
the counts identical, and no number in the engine changes.* That is the disease
this project keeps catching, and it is not about lore.
