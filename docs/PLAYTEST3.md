# PLAYTEST 3 — 2026-08-11, the UI/IA session

The owner played a fresh run and spent most of it lost. Their closing ask:
*"we need to gather all of our features, see where they are, and kinda
reshuffle things, make things more streamlined, clearer… set up lots of agents
to try to build this streamlined experience for the users."*

⚠️ **Everything below is the owner's own words**, lightly de-transcribed.
Agents and future sessions should work from THIS, not from a paraphrase.

## The structural complaint — the one that matters most

> *"Why is people menu and place menu… places are different, right? But people
> is one menu, hero is one menu, log is one menu. Why is it in the same
> selection? I don't understand."*

**Place** is a view of whatever you tapped on the map. **People**, **Hero** and
**Log** are views of the whole town. They are different KINDS of thing and they
were put in one tab row. That is an information-architecture error, and it is
mine.

> *"The top of the screen is very messy. The UI super messy. The things are in
> random places."*

## The war line is in the stats menu

> *"Recoupling camps towards this valley, and we dare you wait to grow. It's a
> stupid thing to have in the same… you know, this is a stats menu. Why is it
> there?"*

The goblin-camps line and the swell warning sit inside the readout strip.
